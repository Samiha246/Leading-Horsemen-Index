## Purpose
The goal of the Leading Horsemen Index is to provide another way of examining market influence by looking beyond a single benchmark and evaluating how companies are weighted across multiple major U.S. indexes.

## Leading-Horsemen-Index
A quantitative framework that identifies the companies with the greatest influence on the U.S. stock market by combining and normalizing the holdings of the S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, and Dow Jones Industrial Average.

## Project Overview
The Leading Horsemen Index combines data from three major U.S. stock market indexes to create a unified ranking of companies based on their combined index weights. The project includes the underlying holdings data, analytical model, research findings, and visual presentation of the index.
